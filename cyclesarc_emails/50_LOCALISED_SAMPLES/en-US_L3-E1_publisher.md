## What the localiser changed, and why

| Layer | Change |
|---|---|
| Spelling | *colors*. |
| Punctuation | The serial comma (*moon, and meteor peak*), which American newspaper people notice when it is missing. |
| Idiom | *at the bottom* for *at the foot*; *typeface* for *type* (which reads as "kind" in US English); *to get started* for *to start*; *the actual thing* for *the thing itself*. |
| Trade word | *masthead* in the proof sentence: the US newspaper word for the thing "your mark" means here. |
| Subject | Names the city: US subjects reward the concrete. |
| Dates and clock | Any date or time in the proof will be *December 14, 2027* and a 12-hour clock in Mountain Time; the data stays metric, and the proof says so once. |
| Notice | Rewritten for CAN-SPAM: sender identified, the physical address in the notice as well as the frame, a working unsubscribe, and the "business email" line kept. No legitimate-interest wording, which means nothing in the US. |
| Send window | Tuesday–Thursday 09:00–11:00 America/Denver; US federal holidays; not Thanksgiving week; not between Christmas and New Year. |
| What did not change | Every fact, the four moves, the single ask, the length (within three words of the master). |

## The master, filled

**Subject:** The sky over Nebra, 1600 BC

Dear Katrin,

Your fulldome show on the Nebra sky disc puts the audience under the sky of
the Mittelberg at around 1600 BC. We can give you the sun, the moon, the
planets and the real star field over the Mittelberg on any date in that
range, with the daylight, the twilight and the horizon of the era, in one
file your renderer reads. Every value carries its source and its uncertainty.

Our proof is Stonehenge, 2500 BC: at midwinter the setting sun sits within a
tenth of a degree of the centre of the Great Trilithon's window. At the
equinox it does not.

Which place and date would you want first? Tell me and I will reply with
what the file holds for that scene and the terms for your production.

Kind regards,

## What the localiser changed, and why

| Layer | Change |
|---|---|
| Register | *Sie* throughout. First contact to a senior person in a German company. |
| Address | *Sehr geehrte Frau Dr. Weber* — surname and academic title; the English "Dear Katrin" would read as presumptuous. |
| Subject | A noun phrase, as German subjects are; *um* for "around", *v. Chr.* for BC. |
| Trade words | *Fulldome-Show* and *Renderer* are the German trade's own words; *Himmelsscheibe von Nebra* and *Mittelberg* are the names a German reader knows. |
| Proof word | *Beleg* (a documented record) rather than *Beweis* (a proof in the logical sense), which would overclaim. |
| The number | "within a tenth of a degree" → *weniger als ein Zehntelgrad … entfernt*; the same number, the same meaning, no rounding. |
| Dates | *2500 v. Chr.*, *1600 v. Chr.* |
| Sign-off | *Mit freundlichen Grüßen*, no comma after it in current German usage. |
| Length | 158 German words against 118 English: inside the ×1.1 allowance plus the longer compound nouns. |
| Notice | Rewritten for the DSGVO: the legal basis (berechtigtes Interesse) named, the right to object stated, the source named. |
| Send window | Tuesday–Thursday 09:00–11:00 Europe/Berlin; calendar: German federal holidays plus Berlin. Not 3 October, not 31 October (Reformation Day in some Länder). |

**Questions for the reviewer:** none. The gender and the title come from the
card. If the card had no title, the greeting would be *Sehr geehrte Frau
Weber*; never a guessed *Dr.*
